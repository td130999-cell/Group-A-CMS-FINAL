== Description ==

Coffee Cafe Corner is designed for small cafes and coffee shops that want a warm and welcoming online presence. It helps you present your menu, special items, opening hours, and location in a clear and friendly way so customers can quickly find what they need. The layout focuses on simplicity, allowing visitors to explore your offerings without distractions while creating a cozy digital experience that reflects your cafe’s atmosphere. It also works smoothly across mobile and desktop devices, making it easy for customers to browse on the go. This theme is built for ease of use, so you can set up your website without technical effort and keep it updated as your menu or details change. It supports essential features like menu sections, image displays, and contact information while keeping everything lightweight and fast. Whether you run a local coffee corner or a growing cafe, it provides a practical and approachable way to connect with customers and share your space online.

= Installation =
	
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in coffee-cafe-corner in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on 'Install' button to install and 'Activate' button to use.
